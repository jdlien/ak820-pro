The commit:

1. A dev-only Diagnostics page, Floor probe (menu/menu.c, under MENU_PROBE = AK820_DEV_HOOKS or MENU_HOST_TEST), in the Write test's place: the Write test (10-27) is removed entirely (its nodes N_WTEST/N_WROW become N_PROBE/N_PROW, since the node enum is at its 64-entry limit; its five title ids become one, T_PROBE, so T_FONT and T_MORE renumber 71->67 and 72->68; its pg.wt state becomes pg.pr {colour, ticks, editing}; wt_* functions, wt_tick's call in menu_tick, env_wt_* in menu_env.h and menu_glue.c, menu_wtest_closed -> menu_probe_closed in menu.h and menu_ops.c). sn32f2xx_pgm_scan itself stays at its default; nothing sets it now.
2. The page's rows: Back; Colour (a click cycles Red, Green, Blue, each applied); Ticks (a click takes the knob: pg.pr.editing, the value shown in angle brackets; turns then step the ticks 0..255 with no wrap and the cursor does not move; a click gives the knob back). Entering the page zeroes pg.pr and applies the dark pattern (every LED at 0, red). pr_apply() calls env_probe(r, g, b) -> rowisr_diag_pattern(254, r, g, b); every way out releases it: pop() -> pr_leave() -> menu_probe_closed() -> env_probe_off() (index 255); menu_return_from_diag() (Fn+D's round trip) and menu_ops.c's per-pass check when no session is open (the idle close at 60 s, a profile key's close, a host restore) call menu_probe_closed(), which releases only when the page set the pattern (pr_live).
3. The rows' signatures: a new exact form for N_PROW rows in row_sig(), in 0x0400-0x0FFF: (val & 3) << 10 | line << 2 | bullet << 1 | lit, where val is the row index (1 Colour, 2 Ticks), line the colour or the ticks, bullet Ticks' capture. The painter (RK_ITEM path) prints the colour's name or the ticks ("%u", or "<%u>" while captured) into its buf.
4. menu_input(): with the page open and editing set, turns go to pr_turn() before the editor/list dispatch.
5. rowisr_diag.c: rowisr_diag_pattern(led, r, g, b) exported (rowisr_diag.h, under AK820_DEV_HOOKS), the sub 3 handler now calls it; a stub when RGB_MATRIX_ENABLE is off.
6. The host test scripts/menu_s0_test.c: the fake env_wt_* replaced by env_probe/env_probe_off fakes, test_wtest replaced by test_probe (it passes, in both the S0 and the -DMENU_ENABLE builds, with gcc -Wall -Wextra).
7. Comment updates in rgb/rowisr.c, rgb/rowisr.h, flash_tidy.h.
