What it does, in graphics/lcd_bus.c: lcd_window_polled() sends the CASET, RASET and RAMWR commands and their parameters as polled sends (spi0_poll_send), toggling DC only between them, each send waiting out BUSY before it returns. lcd_fill_rect_ok(), lcd_stream_begin() and lcd_blit_ram() use it after bus_quiesce(); on a give-up the fill returns false with CS released, the stream marks itself broken (its bytes dropped), and the RAM blit skips its pixels. lcd_blit_flash() (the DMA arm) keeps lcd_window() through spiSend, because it sends its window after spiSN32FlashDmaPrepare() has set SPI0's IE for the DMA.
