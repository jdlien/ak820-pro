The subject: commit 81622b7a43 on the branch row-isr in the worktree qmk_firmware-ak820pro-rowisr, over bfff926086 (stage 5h plus the dev Floor probe page, all reviewed READY on 11-03/04). Read it as

The driver is keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rgb/rowisr.c (the design: plans/ROW-ISR-PLAN.md, D3; the key matrix shares the 15 column pins with the LED matrix, COL2ROW, config.h). The slot handler rowisr_slot() runs once per 125 us slot: step 2 deselects every mux row (the 18 anode rails, 6 key rows x R, B, G), then rowisr_hot(): step 3 on every third slot releases the columns to their pull-ups, drives one key row low, settles 0.5 us, reads the columns, drives the row high and gives the columns back to the PWM; step 4 writes the 15 match registers for the next mux row; step 5 selects that row; step 6 restarts the pulse timers.

The defect, found by JD's eye on the devprobe flavour (a byte = its pulse width in ticks) at 1-12 ticks on 11-04: holding any key brightened the LED above it in its column (for the top row, the bottom row's key), green, only while held, invisible at five ticks and above (so about a tick's worth of light once a field). The mechanism as read from the slot order: the scan of key row k happens in the slot that prepares mux row (k, R), right after step 2 deselected the previous slot's row (k-1, G); that rail's anode line still holds its charge (nothing has drained it: the drain is through an LED whose column goes low, and the scan is the first column-low event after the deselect); a held key at (k, c) pulls column c low through its diode for the scan's microsecond, so the LED at (k-1, c) on the green rail lights from the stored charge.

What the commit does: the slot still scans one key row every third slot, but the row scanned is the one before the row being prepared (scan_row = key_row - 1, wrapping), i.e. the row whose green rail step 2 just deselected, so the ghost lands on the held key's own green, under the finger. rowisr_hot() takes scan_row beside krow (the match registers still use krow, the row being prepared); scan_publish() publishes scan_row. Nothing else changes.
