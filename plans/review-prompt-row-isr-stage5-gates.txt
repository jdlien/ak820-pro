Measured on 5d over 300 s lit at full white: the field 444 Hz (7,997 services a second); the light 92.32 mA against stage 4b's 92.1 (the 61 mA incremental within 0.4%); the handler 49.5 us mean, 32 min, 117 max (page 4, inclusive of preemption, inside the port's prologue and epilogue), 39.6% CPU share; row_gap_max 3 ms; no missed slot, no epoch loss, no spurious invocation; pulses omitted 87 of 2,417,188 (0.0037%), all by the arm deadline (90.5 us), the red slots (the scan slot, colour 0) 3-23 a key row and the others 1-4; the entry latency maximum 74.9 us with the daemon paused and nothing touching the board (the clock's seconds redraw runs on the LCD). On 5c (the terminal 2,047, the deadline 80 us, the settle 2 us) the omissions were 2,311 (0.096%), every one a red slot. The presser's short taps: exact at 10 ms and up, 88 of 100 at 8 ms (72 on stage 3 at the 267 us cadence; the debounce is 5 ms sym_defer_pk). The camera gate at 125 us: two forced clock-chip writes filmed, nothing in the writer's windows.

The plan's stage 5 gates say: ~8,000 serviced slots a second and 444 samples a second a row (met); the handler's elapsed share <= 20% and its latency tail bounded (NOT met: 39.6%); row_gap_max <= 4 ms (met); omissions zero in quiet runs and under 0.1% under mixed load (NOT met to the letter: 87 in 300 s in a quiet run with the LCD's clock running); the light within 5% (met); the lowest codes by camera and the witness (not measured: no witness exists yet); stage 4's clock gates, the wireless gates and the sleep/wake gates repeated (the camera gate met; the wakes and the silence set owed, with Buzz locked).
