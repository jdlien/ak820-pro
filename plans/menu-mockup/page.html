AK820 Config Menu

The knob menu as built (the firmware of 10-27), drawn pixel for pixel on the board's 128 × 128 screen from the board's own font table, colours and layouts; every page was checked against frames the firmware's menu code draws on the host test's panel. The Face switch puts the same menu in an 8 × 16 font instead, laid out for 15 columns and 7 rows, to compare sizes. Press Fn + click to close it and see the dashboard; press it again to reopen.

Painting

Board speed paints a changed row in pieces, about 9 ms each, the old highlight's removal before the new one, as the firmware's scheduler does: a move between rows takes ~165 ms. Judge the grey and the yellow on the panel itself, since its greys read darker than a monitor's.

Dina is Cozette's parent font. Every 8×16 face keeps lowercase at 7 px: they gain most in spacing and stroke weight, not height. The 8×16 icons are redrawn at about 9 to 11 px, from Cozette's 7 px originals (the Icons switch shows those, centred in the bigger cells).

The 8×16 text pass

  • Always shorter: Effect → FX, Second color → 2nd color, Lock lights → Lock LEDs, Bluetooth 1 → BT 1, Pair device to BT 1 → Pair to BT 1, Save current here → Save here, Hours of use → Hours, Reset settings → Defaults, Reset counters → Reset counts, Using the menu → Menu basics.
  • Only where a row won't fit: Connection → Link, Percent → %.
  • Top level: Power's value is its mode's bulb; with icons, Profiles shows the profile in use by name; page one ends at Battery.
  • Pages: the Clock's formats show as their examples; a profile's summary is two lines (the screen and clock line goes); Help and the confirms are reworded for 15 columns; About scrolls.

Driving it

Turn the knob← → or ↑ ↓, scroll over the screen, or drag the knob round
ClickEnter or Space, or tap the knob
Hold (back, cancel)Backspace, or press and hold the knob
Fn + click (open, close)Esc or F
Rename a profileType: the keys go to the name, Enter saves, Esc cancels

Saved on the board

Lighting, the screen and the indicators preview as you turn; only a click saves, and holding or closing mid-edit puts the old value back. Power, Clock and Battery apply on the click. Resting 0.3 s on a profile previews its lighting and screen level on the keys.

Where this differs from the board

  • Health page and Camera page hand the screen to Fn+D's pages; here they're illustrative. A dev build's Diagnostics adds a Write test row, left out here.
  • The dashboard behind the menu is approximate. The menu's pages are exact.
  • Pairing and slot changes are simulated: a slot links 1.5 s after a click, and pairing waits until you stop it.
  • Not built yet, so not shown: About's second page with the bunny, and Help's QR code.
  • The 8×16 faces are a proposal: no firmware draws them yet.
