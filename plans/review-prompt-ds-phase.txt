THE INCIDENT. On 10-28 at ~23:02 gremlin's AK820 Pro (firmware c4bc0852, dev) took a genuine watchdog reset (RSTST 0x03) about 8 minutes into an untouched deep sleep: on battery, slider on BT, the host downstairs, the USB cable fully out. The retained watchdog record (watchdog_record.c, the 16 B .ram7 region) said only site 27 WDT_SITE_DEEP_SLEEP within housekeeping, and every other fact of that sleep (sleep.c's wake ring, stray_diag, ds_* statics) was in .bss and was zeroed at boot. Static reading found nothing in deep_sleep() that can wait unboundedly; the candidates are (a) a wedge at the wake's first flash fetch after the WFI (the errata-37 class in sleep.c's comment above ds_deep_wfi), (b) an interrupt storm or wedged ISR the moment chSysUnlock unmasks, (c) the WDT counting asleep with no counted second.

THE CHANGE. Firmware: qmk_firmware-ak820pro, branch gremlin/ds-phase, commits 86663384 and 27d1105d on top of 8ec0c8b9 (git diff 8ec0c8b9 27d1105d). Host side: the outer repo's commit e5e019e (ak820-agent/src/health.rs, tests/watchdog_record_test.c).
1. All builds: deep_sleep()'s phases stored straight into the retained path word: sites 28 DS_ASLEEP (before each WFI), 29 DS_WAKING (stored inside ds_deep_wfi, in SRAM, right after the WFI and the PMU write, before the ~108 us wait and the flash read), 30 DS_RESTORE (from the loop's exit through the clock restore), 31 DS_UNLOCKED (just before chSysUnlock to the return); and the scope's own word (site 27) after each wake. New watchdog_record_word() and watchdog_record_path(). Each store is made twice: on this part the last SRAM write before a stall can be lost at the reset (see commit_terminal's comment in watchdog_record.c). Sites 32+ belong to the main loop's sites (ak820pro.h's loop_site enum), so only 28-31 were free.
2. Dev builds only (AK820_DEV_HOOKS): the sleep under way kept through a reset, as HC_DEVHOOK op 24 slot 9 (layout in sleep.h); the wake ring and stray_diag moved into .ram0's no-init part beside it (ChibiOS rules_memory.ld's .ram0 output section: crt1.c clears only __ram0_clear__..__ram0_noinit__). Trusted at boot only when watchdog_boot_rstst() shows no POR or LVD and the magic matches; otherwise zeroed. The ELF of the dev build places ds_last 0x20007e28, stray_diag 0x20007e44, wake_seq 0x20007e60, wake_ring 0x20007e64, __ram0_free__ 0x20007f34; ram7 starts at 0x20007FF0, the DFU magic at __ram0_end__ - 4.
