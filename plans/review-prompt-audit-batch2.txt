Deliberate choices to check rather than accept:
- F-005: rtc_init() reads Control_status_1 before the seed and clears STOP; it then marks the PCF stale with no_retry (pcf_sync_host_skipped), so neither the discipline nor the acquisition takes it as a reference and it is not written back unasked until a host sets the time. A failed release goes to PS_RECOVER with stop_asserted set. Check the interactions with the deep sleep's wake (wake_coarse uses the PCF's elapsed time), the acquisition, and pcf_sync_retry_due; and whether rtc_bus_guard() is safe this early in boot.
- F-006: the seed retry sits in rtc_task() after its guards and the 60 s check, before the REF_SOF return.
- F-025: inside the step's lock, a latched SECIF is cleared with the NVIC pending bit and counted, not served. Check against the LLD's handler (lib/chibios-contrib/os/hal/ports/SN32/LLD/SN32F2xx/RTC/hal_rtc_lld.c) and rtc_second_cb's other per-second work that is skipped this way.
- F-026: the slew's start is compensated only when the next tick rewrites the register; interval_cycles accounting follows.
