What changed:
1. The scan (D3 step 3): the key row selected by palClearLine (one BCLR) and deselected by palSetLine (one BSET); no gpio_set_pin_output on the hot path any more (the interim scanner re-asserted output mode with a read-modify-write of the port's MODE on every scan). 4. frmno_read(): FRMNO read twice and a third time if the two differ; used in frm_staleI, frm_trackI, the exit's frm_x and the record. The reason: pairs of false frame-counter rejections at spans 0/256 ms (usb_last_dt_ms 0, usb_last_df_ms 256), twice on the trim build in ordinary running (2 of 165,641 judged) and twice in the exit-only build's battery 20 ms silence case tonight, never in ~450,000 other judged intervals: our reading is a torn read at the carry into bit 8 (0x0FF read as 0x000 gives that interval -255 = 1,793 mod 2,048, and the next interval, from the torn baseline the rejection path adopts, +256; both rejected, two false losses).
5. A dev scan of FRMNO (sub 14, fscan_slice/fscan_fill in rowisr_diag.c): the housekeeping task reads the register back to back 3 ms a pass until the asked time (up to 60 s), classing every step as 0, +1 or other, keeping three (prev, next) pairs.
6. The handler's sections (sub 11) are opt-in by ROWISR_SECTIONS (default 0 in rowisr.h): their commit after stamp 6 cost ~6 us a slot, which page 4's hooks saw and which hid the exit-only build's 6.6 us saving from the warm windows.
