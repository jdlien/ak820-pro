Context. D3 case 5 is the USB frame-counter detector: on USB, an interval's span by the CT16B5 tick extension and its span by the host's SOF frame counter (FRMNO, 11 bits) must agree modulo 2.048 s within 3 ms plus 0.2%, which catches a returning silence of every observer that the 8 ms guard cannot see (349.5 ms and its multiples, the extension's wrap). Until this commit the handler judged every service's interval against the previous service's snapshot (last_snap), with a full snapshot (the extension, the TC, the generation, FRMNO, the bus-event generation, the bus state from three USB registers, the freshness) at every entry and every exit, and a second judgment at the exit on the handler's own span. Measured on the board on 11-03: those two snapshots and two judgments cost 31 us of an 80 us slot, with no hot spot (the sections 7.3, 9.6, 5.8, 8.7 us), a register read costing nothing over RAM; it is the instruction count at 3-5 cycles each from flash. Stage 2 without the detector was 59.8 us.

The commit: the judgment runs every eighth service (USB_JUDGE_EVERY, usb_phase), on the interval since the last judged service, against a second baseline usb_base (the last judged service's full snapshot); the seven services between take take_timeI (no FRMNO, no bus state, no freshness). The judged interval must be continuous in the extension exactly as the per-slot interval must (one generation, VALID at both ends, under EPOCH_CONSUMER_MAX) and begun with the frames expected (usb_base.bus && (usb_base.fresh || usb_base.pending)). The baseline moves to the judging service whether the interval was judged, rejected, unjudged or not continuous; after the service's own rejection it takes adopt_gen, as service_commit's last_snap does. The exit snapshot is take_timeI and the exit judgment is removed (dur_ok is the generation check alone). The per-slot baseline last_snap, the spurious check, boundaries_between, the generation accounting, the guard and the tick's observer are unchanged. Note: on a spurious invocation (boundaries == 0) the handler returns before the baseline moves, so that judged interval spans sixteen services, still continuous.
