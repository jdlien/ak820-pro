What it does: dev op 28 sub 3 (rowisr_diag.c, "one LED, one colour": a pattern applied after each render in rgb_matrix_indicators_kb, one LED at r,g,b and every other dark) now also takes LED index 254 for every LED at that colour (rgb_matrix_set_color_all); 255 stays off, 0..80 one LED. The host's `led all R G B` sends 254 and prints the echo. The purpose: stage 6's floor measurement per colour on the devprobe flavour (build.sh: -DROWISR_K_Q6=64u, so a byte is its pulse width in ticks at the 125 us cadence, full scale 255 ticks), with every LED held at N ticks on one colour so the eye in a dark room, the camera and the INA228 logger (which needs the sum of 81 LEDs to resolve ~0.2 mA at 10 ticks) read the same light (plans/ROW-ISR-PLAN.md, "Stage 6: the precision path", "The floor is measured first, per colour").
