The model against the plan as written: the states (MODEL, UNKNOWN-CHG, LOST, OVERRUN, FULL), FROM-FLAT, the pause and its handover, the re-seat after the unplug (owed, RELAX_S, below and at the clamp, LOST as UNKNOWN, a replug keeps it owed), the ceiling U from fresh estimates below the clamp, the rise limiter, the 990 cap, FULL. 2. Trial 1's numbers. K_CC came out at 133 (122-142), two ways that agree. B1 had bounded it at 165-213 by assuming 70-90% of a charge goes in before the knee, the VDD rise at ~4.24 h. With L_KNEE = K_CC x t_knee - R the knee is 533, so the tail carries half the charge (tau 5.78 h), and it is still rising at 54 pm/h when it reaches 990 at T_TAIL. The plan says that if B3 contradicts B1 beyond the uncertainty: "revise the model (the knee, not the measurements), or ship flash 2 with 'Charge' for every session plus the re-seat". Is shipping the model at these numbers, marked provisional until trial 2, defensible? Which way does its display err if the charger really stays at constant current past the VDD rise, and by how much? Is anything worse than the placeholder tail (K 190, L_KNEE 775) it replaces? Or should flash 2 show "Charge" for every session until the knee is measured (a bench logger with a current sensor is on order)?

3. RELAX_S and PAUSE_S at 1800 s (they were 120 and 600). Is any timer, product or comparison keyed on them sized or written for short values (u16 seconds, ms products, LOST_S or STALE_LOST_S interplay)? 4. D, the histogram trimmed mean: is it exactly the old sort's result (eviction, c5_forget, the trim counts at every ring fill, the rounding)? Can the counts or the sum overflow?

5. C2, the RTC persist scheduler: the first save at 10 min only when the stored period is outside 28000-40000; then saves only when the period is >= 64 ticks off AND >= 6 h since the last. Can it write more than the plan's budget (<= 10 physical write sessions a day on battery)? Does it ever skip a needed first save, or save a period that is not converged?
