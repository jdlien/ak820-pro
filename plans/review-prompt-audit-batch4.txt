Deliberate choices to check rather than accept:
- F-001: the SPI stays per byte (ChibiOS calls); only the pass budget changed. An erase, a page program or a CRC slice spends the pass (flash_channel_pass_end() in housekeeping clears it); the rest answer FS_BUSY. Check that every host that drives the flash channel (ak820ctl's provisioning, its CRC check, anything in hostagent/ or scripts/ that sends these commands) retries FS_BUSY rather than failing, that a busy answer can never be taken for a result, and that the watchdog is fed through the longest path that remains.
- F-044: FC_CRC32 and FC_CRC_NEXT answer FS_BUSY while the chip reports write-in-progress. Check every command that can follow a page program or an erase.
- F-018: the index's entries are checked against the panel (128x128) and the chip, and refused entries are compacted out. Check the ids the firmware uses after compaction (asset ids are assigned by sorted filename; docs/fonts-assets.md), every caller of the index, and the shipped assets' real sizes.
- F-014: the blit is reported lost once the chip reads idle. Check who reads the report and what it does.
- ak820ctl: the erase now runs from the top sector down. Check why the audit wanted that, and every exit path for the unlock.
