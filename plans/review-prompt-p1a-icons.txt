(4) Row content: an icon in cells 0-1 of each top-level row (a one-cell icon then a space, or a two-cell icon), its label at cell 2; "◀ Back" for "‹ Back"; ▶ for ">" on rows that open a page; the saved option's mark is the two-cell circled check where it was the one-cell bullet, its label still at cell 2.
