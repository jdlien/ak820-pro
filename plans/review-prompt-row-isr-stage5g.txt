Context. D3 case 5 is the USB frame-counter detector: on USB, an interval's span by the CT16B5 tick extension and its span by the host's SOF frame counter (FRMNO, 11 bits) must agree modulo 2.048 s within 3 ms plus 0.2%, which catches a returning silence of every observer that the 8 ms guard cannot see (349.5 ms and its multiples, the extension's wrap). Until this commit the handler judged every service's interval against the previous service's snapshot (last_snap). The board runs the 125 us slot (stage 5e, 739fe1b635): the handler's mean is 49.5-52.4 us, 40-42% of the core, and the judgment's bookkeeping is the last cheap lever (the SRAM trial, 5f, gave nothing). The commit. Every service still takes the full snapshot (take_snapI: FRMNO tracked, usb_gen, the bus state, the freshness, the recovery flag): the evidence is kept at every service. The judgment is decimated: (1) every USB_JUDGE_EVERY-th (8) counted service (a spurious invocation returns before the count) judges the interval usb_base -> s1 with usb_judge(), and the baseline moves to s1 whatever the verdict (after its own rejection it takes adopt_gen, as service_commit's last_snap does); (2) at ANY service, before that, a discontinuity (have_usb_base and: s1 INVALID, or s1.gen != usb_base.gen, or the span over an hour) first judges the pending interval usb_base -> last_snap, if last_snap is VALID, in usb_base's generation, later than usb_base and within the hour (a crossed service commits nothing, so last_snap may be the baseline itself, excluded by ext equality, or older, excluded by the wrapped span), then gives the baseline up; (3) with no baseline, the first VALID service becomes it (usb_phase reset, the pending sums dropped). usb_judge(a, b) is the per-slot verdict as a pure function of the two snapshots (UNTRUSTED when a's bus is down or frames known stopped: not judged; REJECT on a bus event or the frames stopping or the spans disagreeing; UNJUDGED for both ends in a recovery; KEPT otherwise), updating the record's usb_* counts as before. At a judging service a REJECT invalidates the extension exactly as the per-slot rejection did (the fresh check, time_invalidateI(false), adopt_gen, seen_gen, adopt_pending, one loss, valid1 false). At a discontinuity a REJECT counts one loss (sat8 epoch_losses) and moves nothing: the generation has moved already, and that move was counted by the entry's generation check, so the entry can count two losses (the gap's and the alias's). The interval statistics service_commit kept (slots_elapsed, serviced_valid, latency_max) now go to pending sums unless the service's own interval was judged here and kept (direct); the pending sums are folded at a KEPT or UNTRUSTED verdict, dropped at REJECT or UNJUDGED and at a new baseline. The USB baseline is NOT reset at the three have_last resets: the scan-only switch (no generation change; the judgment is of time, not slots) and the deep sleep's entry and exit (the wake's first service finds the generation moved by the exclusion and judges the pre-sleep pending interval from the kept snapshots; SRAM is retained through the deep sleep). rowisr_diag.c's silence report now waits after the 2 ms until the judging-opportunity counter (usb_judge_seq, bumped at every judgment, deferred or not, whatever the verdict) has advanced by two since before the masked spin, 20 ms at most, and reports the count and the wait in sub 9 [16..17]. The host (scripts/rowisr_diag.py, this repository's main tree) parses the two bytes.
