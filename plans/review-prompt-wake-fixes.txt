WHAT IT IS FOR (plans/AUDIT-2026-10-31.md has F-045 and F-032; plans/GREMLIN-RESULTS.md section 21 has the measurements)
1. F-045: deep_sleep() used to set USB_CFG.PHY_EN after every wake (usb_phy_onI). The SN32 USB LLD (lib/chibios-contrib/os/hal/ports/SN32/LLD/SN32F2xx/USB/hal_usb_lld.c) clears PHY_EN and ESD_EN together at BUS_SUSPEND and sets both at resume/wakeup. After a cable-out wake the board was left with PHY_EN on and ESD_EN off (seen twice on hardware). The fix takes the PHY_EN|ESD_EN pair under the lock before the teardown and restores only bits that were set then; the dev light sleep (light_task) does the same with a copy taken as its PMU/WFI bits begin.
2. The CT16B3 counter reset: gptStartContinuousI -> gpt_lld_start_timer -> CT16B3_ResetTimer() spins on TMRCTRL.CRST with no bound (lib/chibios-contrib/os/hal/ports/SN32/LLD/SN32F2xx/CT/sn32_ct.h and hal_gpt_lld.c), under the lock, at every deep sleep's wake. pwm_tick_startI() now performs the same reset first with a bound (~0.5 ms); if it does not complete it writes a terminal watchdog record (site 69) and resets.
3. F-032: the three bounded clock waits in deep_sleep() reset the board with NVIC_SystemReset, and a software reset's boot discarded the record. Now each writes a terminal record (site 68, a PC word naming the wait and CSST) first, and watchdog_record_boot() keeps a FAULT_MAGIC record with site 68 or 69 when RSTST has SWRSTF, without raising the consecutive-reset count.
