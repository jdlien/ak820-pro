Context. This repository is a QMK/ChibiOS firmware for the Ajazz AK820 Pro keyboard on a Sonix SN32F299 (Cortex-M0, 48 MHz, 256 KB flash, 32 KB SRAM). Its 81 RGB LEDs are a multiplexed matrix driven by an interrupt on timer CT16B1, called "the row ISR" throughout the docs, in qmk_firmware-ak820pro/drivers/led/sn32f2xx.c (a QMK core file carrying local commits), configured by qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/config.h and mcuconf.h. The same ISR scans the key matrix, because the LED columns and the key columns share pins. Measured on the board: the ISR takes about 73% of the CPU and the LED field rate is 215 Hz. There are a few visible flicker and dimming events the owner still sees: an internal-flash cleanup, the clock chip's I2C write, and LCD fills. The owner wants the lights smoother, cheaper in CPU, and more adjustable (finer dimming at the dim end, where the board is used; better colour control), without losing keystrokes or regressing the wireless link, and wants a rewrite and optimization of this driver designed properly before anything is built.
