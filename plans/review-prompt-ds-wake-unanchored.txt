THE INCIDENT (10-28, firmware c4bc0852, a "dev" build):
- The board had been on USB (cable plugged into a Windows PC), idle long enough that the idle ladder's sleep time had passed while on USB (on USB it does not sleep). The user pulled the cable at 22:54:38. The board entered deep sleep about 2 s later: a pending write to the PCF real-time-clock chip over I2C went first, then the sleep began. Power mode Balanced. Slider on Bluetooth; the Bluetooth host (the PC) was then two floors away, so the link was weak or lost. On battery. Nobody touched it.
- At ~23:02 the user saw the firmware's boot banner reporting a watchdog reset.
- The retained watchdog record (survives reset, see watchdog_record.c) read: valid; site 27 (WDT_SITE_DEEP_SLEEP) within housekeeping; RSTST 0x03 (WDTRSTF set, SWRSTF at its reset value: a genuine watchdog reset; a software NVIC_SystemReset reads 0x01); consecutive resets 1; last_pass_uptime_ms 7,399,572.
- The firmware's uptime counter froze at the sleep's entry (it does not advance asleep), and the post-reset uptime puts the reset at ~23:02:32: one sleep of ~8 minutes, and no main-loop pass between the sleep's entry and the reset.

MEASURED SINCE (facts, not theories):
- The watchdog does not count during Deep-sleep on this chip: three sleeps armed with no watchdog feed ran 120, 120 and 600 RTC-counted seconds against an ~11.5 s timeout, with no reset.
- Asleep, the RTC's second runs ~13-15% slow.
- Hundreds of deep sleeps on another unit of the same keyboard (247/247 one night, soaks of 97/100, 139/150; the failures were unrelated UART overruns and display paints) never produced a watchdog reset; nearly all had the USB cable plugged into a powered-off hub port (power and data cut by a relay and uhubctl, so D+/D- stayed connected to the hub), or the host nearby. That unit also ran 41 hours, ~31 h asleep, 32+ sleeps, several with the cable fully out of the keyboard, with no reset.
- The unit that reset had slept with its cable fully out 6 times before, each woken on purpose within minutes, with the host near. Since then it ran three more cable-out sleeps cleanly, including one entered ~2 s after a pull with the ladder past on USB (as on 10-28), but that one was woken after ~95 s.
- So the reset has been seen once, on one unit, in a sleep of ~8 minutes entered just after a cable pull, with the Bluetooth host far away.
