Two concrete remaining P2 counterexamples:

- **Generation alias:** 65,536 cycles of an 8 ms invalidating observation gap followed by a short re-prime fit in roughly **527 seconds**. The generation returns to its original value. Because rejected gaps are omitted from the extension, its delta also passes the consumer bound. Sixteen bits therefore do not establish the claimed hour-long protection.
- **Wrapped mean:** ten million samples of 480 ticks each represent a true **10 µs** mean. After the sum wraps, Python reports approximately **1.05 µs**. Use modular deltas over a bounded window, or widen the sum.

Allowing either observer to re-prime after wake is a reasonable adjustment to finding 6: generation checks prevent reconstruction across the sleep loss. The remaining problem is the inconsistent treatment of validity later in the service.
